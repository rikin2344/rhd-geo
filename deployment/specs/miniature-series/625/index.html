⚡Load Ratings

Dynamic Load (Cr) 1.88kN
Static Load (Cor) 0.68kN
Load Capacity 192kg force

💡What This Means

Dynamic Load (1.88kN = 192kg): Maximum force while rotating. Exceeding this causes rapid wear and failure within hours.
Static Load (0.68kN): Maximum force when stationary. Use 80% for safety.
Calculate Your Load: Measure shaft forces, belt tensions, or gear loads. Include shock/vibration factors.
Failure Warning: Overloading causes bearing raceways to crack and balls to flatten permanently.

🔄Speed Limits

Grease Lubrication 32000 RPM
Oil Lubrication 40000 RPM
Recommended Max 28800 RPM (90%)

💡What This Means

Grease Limit (32000 RPM): Heat generation increases exponentially above this. Grease breaks down, causing seizure.
Oil Advantage (40000 RPM): Better heat dissipation allows higher speed. Required for high-speed spindles.
Safe Operating Speed: Use 90% of limit (28800 RPM) for maximum L10 life of 10,000+ hours.
Failure Mode: Exceeding speed causes cage failure, ball skidding, and catastrophic overheating within minutes.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Expert guidance for 625 bearing applications

Bearing Selection & Replacement ▼

How do I know if the 625 bearing is the right size for my 5mm shaft?

The 625 bearing has a 5mm bore diameter with h6 tolerance (±0.011mm), making it perfect for 5mm shafts with standard tolerance requirements.
Why This Matters
Proper shaft fit prevents premature wear, vibration, and bearing failure. h6 tolerance ensures optimal interference fit for most applications.
How To Handle It
Measure your shaft diameter with a micrometer. If it's 5mm ±0.011mm, the 625 bearing will fit perfectly. For tighter tolerances, consider h5 or h4 shaft grades.
From 20+ years of manufacturing experience: Always measure both shaft and housing before ordering. 80% of bearing failures come from improper sizing.

What's the difference between 625 and 626 bearings for my precision application?

625 has 5mm bore (16x5mm), 626 has 6mm bore (19x6mm). 625 is more compact but 626 offers higher load capacity (192 kg vs. 286 kg).
Why This Matters
Space constraints vs. load requirements determine your choice. 625 is ideal for ultra-compact designs, 626 for higher load applications.
How To Handle It
Measure available space and calculate load requirements. If space is critical and loads are under 192 kg, choose 625. If you need higher capacity, choose 626.
Our precision engineering team recommends 625 for medical devices and 626 for industrial instruments where space allows.

Can I use the 625 bearing in high-temperature applications?

Standard 625 bearings operate from -20°C to +120°C. For high-temperature use, we offer C3 clearance variants that handle up to +150°C.
Why This Matters
Temperature affects bearing clearance, lubrication, and material properties. High temperatures can cause premature failure without proper clearance.
How To Handle It
For temperatures above 120°C, specify C3 clearance when ordering. Above 150°C, consider special high-temperature materials and lubrication.
Our ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing includes temperature testing to ensure bearings meet specified operating ranges.
Installation & Maintenance ▼

What's the proper installation method for the 625 bearing in a precision instrument?

Use a precision bearing press or induction heater for installation. Apply force to the outer ring only, never to the inner ring or balls.
Why This Matters
Improper installation damages bearing surfaces, reduces precision, and shortens service life. Precision instruments require sub-micron accuracy.
How To Handle It
Clean shaft and housing thoroughly. Heat housing to 80-100°C or use precision press. Install bearing with gentle, even pressure on outer ring.
Our manufacturing process includes 100% dimensional testing. Professional installation tools prevent 80% of premature bearing failures.

How often should I lubricate the 625 bearing in continuous operation?

Open bearings require external lubrication every 3-6 months. Shielded/sealed bearings are lubricated for life.
Why This Matters
Proper lubrication prevents wear, reduces friction, and extends bearing life. Insufficient lubrication causes 60% of bearing failures.
How To Handle It
For open bearings, use high-quality grease compatible with operating temperature. For sealed bearings, no additional lubrication needed.
Our Gcr15 steel composition provides superior wear resistance, but proper lubrication is still essential for maximum 50,000-hour service life.

What tools do I need for proper 625 bearing installation and maintenance?

Essential tools include: precision bearing press, induction heater, micrometer, dial indicator, and proper cleaning supplies.
Why This Matters
Proper tools ensure accurate installation, prevent damage, and maintain precision. Precision bearings require professional-grade equipment.
How To Handle It
Invest in quality tools or use professional installation services. For critical applications, consider our installation training programs.
Our technical support team provides installation guidance and can recommend specific tools for your application requirements.
Troubleshooting & Problem Solving ▼

My 625 bearing is making unusual noise. What could be causing this?

Unusual noise usually indicates contamination, improper installation, or excessive load. Check for dirt, verify installation, and confirm load is under 192 kg.
Why This Matters
Noise indicates bearing damage or improper operation. Early detection prevents catastrophic failure and maintains precision.
How To Handle It
Inspect for contamination, verify shaft and housing tolerances, check load calculations, and ensure proper lubrication. Replace if damage is found.
Our vibration analysis shows 625 bearings should operate below 58 μm/s² (V2) for optimal performance. Higher levels indicate problems.

The 625 bearing is running hot during operation. What should I check?

Check lubrication, load, speed, and clearance. Ensure load is under 192 kg, speed under 32,000 RPM, and proper grease is used.
Why This Matters
Excessive heat damages bearing materials, reduces precision, and shortens life. Heat indicates friction and potential failure.
How To Handle It
Verify load calculations, check lubrication quantity and type, ensure proper clearance, and monitor operating speed.
Our Gcr15 steel composition handles temperatures up to 120°C, but optimal operation is below 80°C for maximum life.

How can I prevent premature failure of the 625 bearing in my application?

Prevent failure by: proper installation, correct load (under 192 kg), adequate lubrication, contamination control, and regular inspection.
Why This Matters
Preventive maintenance extends bearing life 3-10x and prevents costly equipment downtime. Most failures are preventable.
How To Handle It
Follow installation procedures, monitor operating conditions, maintain clean environment, and inspect regularly for wear signs.
Our quality assurance process includes statistical process control. Premium bearings last 3-10x longer than economy alternatives.
