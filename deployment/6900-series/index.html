Medium-Duty Compact Excellence

RHD's V3 Ultra and V4 Max 6900 series bearings are specifically engineered for medium-duty applications where space efficiency meets performance requirements. Our medium thin section design provides the optimal balance between load capacity and compact size, making these bearings ideal for industrial automation, packaging machinery, and compact motor assemblies. With excellent speed capability up to 52,000 RPM and load capacities reaching 33.7kN, these bearings bridge the gap between ultra-lightweight and heavy-duty solutions. From conveyor systems to compact gearboxes, RHD 6900 series bearings deliver the reliable performance that medium-duty applications demand while maintaining the space efficiency that modern equipment designs require.
