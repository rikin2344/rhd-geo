Ultra-Precision Equipment Excellence

RHD's V3 Ultra and V4 Max 62300 series bearings are specifically engineered for ultra-precision applications where maximum radial stiffness is paramount. Our extra wide inner ring design provides unprecedented shaft support and minimal deflection under load, making these bearings the preferred choice for coordinate measuring machines, ultra-precision machining centers, and advanced research equipment. With precision grades up to P2 super precision and ceramic hybrid options available, these bearings enable sub-micron accuracy in the most demanding applications. From semiconductor fabrication tools to quantum research instruments, RHD 62300 series bearings provide the ultimate combination of stiffness, precision, and reliability that critical applications require.
