Space-Critical Equipment Excellence

RHD's V3 Ultra and V4 Max 16000 series bearings are specifically engineered for space-critical applications where every millimeter matters. Our advanced thin section design and precision manufacturing deliver exceptional performance in compact equipment from robotic systems to aerospace applications. With optimized geometries that maximize load capacity while minimizing radial space requirements, these bearings enable innovative equipment designs that were previously impossible. From medical devices and portable equipment to drone systems and compact automation, RHD thin section bearings provide the space-saving solutions that modern engineering demands while maintaining the reliability and durability that critical applications require.
