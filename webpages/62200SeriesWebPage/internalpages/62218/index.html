💡What This Means

Dynamic Load (70.0kN): Maximum force while rotating. Exceeding this causes rapid wear and failure within hours.
Static Load (28.5kN): Maximum force when stationary. Use 80% for safety.
Calculate Your Load: Measure shaft forces, belt tensions, or gear loads. Include shock/vibration factors.
Failure Warning: Overloading causes bearing raceways to crack and balls to flatten permanently.

Failure Mode: Exceeding speed causes cage failure, ball skidding, and catastrophic overheating within minutes.

💡What This Means

C2 (Tight): Precision applications - less play, more accurate
C0 (Normal): Standard choice - works for most applications
C3 (Loose): Better for hot environments - allows thermal expansion
C4 (Exta Loose): Hot or tight-fit setups — extra room for thermal expansion.
C5 (Max Loose): Extreme heat/tight fits or near-limit speeds — maximum expansion allowance

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Expert guidance for 62218 bearing applications

Bearing Selection & Replacement ▼

Can a 62218 bearing handle 7000kg loads in mining equipment?

Yes, the 62218 can safely handle 7000kg loads with 140kg (2%) safety margin for mining applications.
Why This Matters
Mining equipment subjects bearings to extreme loads, shock, vibration, and contamination. The 62218's 7140kg capacity provides adequate margin for shock loads and misalignment common in mining operations. The extended 40mm width provides stability under multi-directional loading.
How To Handle It
Use sealed versions (62218-2RS) for contaminated mining environments. Implement robust lubrication systems and condition monitoring. Consider C3 clearance for thermal expansion from heavy loads and high temperatures.
Mining applications benefit from automatic lubrication systems - reduces maintenance and extends bearing life significantly.

What's the difference between 62218 and standard 6218 bearings?

62218 has 40mm width vs 6218's 30mm width, providing significantly higher load capacity in same bore size.
Why This Matters
The extra 10mm width increases load capacity from 9771.6kg (95.8kN) to 70.0kN dynamic load rating. This makes 62218 ideal for extreme load applications without changing shaft diameter. The wider design provides exceptional stability under combined loads and shock conditions.
How To Handle It
Verify available space - ensure 40mm width fits your application. Check shaft shoulder dimensions and housing depth requirements. Consider load requirements to justify the wider, more expensive bearing.
62218 costs 40% more than 6218 but handles 50% higher loads - excellent value for extreme-duty applications.

Is 62218 suitable for 90mm heavy construction equipment shafts?

Excellent choice for heavy construction equipment requiring maximum load capacity on 90mm shafts.
Why This Matters
Heavy construction equipment like large excavators and dozers subject bearings to extreme loads, shock, and harsh environments. The 62218's 7140kg capacity and rugged construction handle the most demanding construction applications with the extended width providing stability.
How To Handle It
Mandate sealed versions (62218-2RS) for contaminated environments. Ensure robust filtration and lubrication systems. Consider automatic greasing systems for continuous operation and reduced maintenance.
Construction equipment demands maximum protection - 62218-2RS with automatic lubrication provides 5+ year service life.
Installation & Maintenance ▼

What tools are needed to install a 3.22kg 62218 bearing properly?

Heavy-duty hydraulic puller, induction heater, and precision alignment tools for proper 90mm bore installation.
Why This Matters
Proper installation prevents 80% of premature bearing failures. The 3.22kg weight and 90mm bore with 40mm width require specialized heavy-duty tools to avoid damage. The extended width makes precise alignment critical to prevent edge loading and internal stress.
How To Handle It
Heat bearing to 80-100°C using induction heater for even heating. Use heavy-duty hydraulic tools for installation and removal. Verify shaft tolerances (h6: 89.987-90.013mm) and housing concentricity. Never use impact tools.
Laser alignment tools ensure precision installation - critical for bearings operating at high loads and speeds.

How often should I inspect a 62218 bearing running at 3400 RPM?

Daily inspections for bearings operating at 3400 RPM in extreme-duty applications.
Why This Matters
Operating at 94% of rated speed with extreme loads requires constant monitoring. The combination of high loads and speeds generates significant heat and stress. Daily monitoring prevents catastrophic failures that can destroy expensive equipment.
How To Handle It
Monitor temperature, vibration, and noise levels daily. Implement continuous condition monitoring systems where possible. Check lubrication condition and contamination weekly. Document all measurements for trending analysis.
Wireless condition monitoring systems provide 24/7 surveillance - essential for preventing catastrophic failures in critical equipment.

What lubrication schedule works for 62218 in extreme-duty applications?

Relubricate every 500-1000 operating hours depending on load, contamination, and environmental conditions.
Why This Matters
Extreme-duty applications generate massive heat and stress requiring frequent lubrication. The 62218's 7140kg capacity and harsh operating conditions accelerate lubricant degradation. Contamination can destroy bearings rapidly without proper lubrication management.
How To Handle It
Use extreme-pressure lithium complex grease for severe applications. Implement automatic lubrication systems where possible. Monitor grease condition continuously and purge contaminated lubricant immediately. Filter all lubricant supplies.
Synthetic lubricants extend service intervals to 2000+ hours while providing superior protection in extreme conditions.
Troubleshooting ▼

Why does my 62218 bearing overheat under 6800kg loads?

6800kg is 95% of capacity - verify lubrication, alignment, and consider enhanced cooling systems.
Why This Matters
Operating at 95% of rated capacity generates extreme heat, especially with any lubrication or alignment issues. The minimal safety margin leaves no room for contamination, thermal effects, or shock loads that increase effective loading beyond design limits.
How To Handle It
Verify actual loads including all dynamic and shock factors. Check alignment within 0.013mm tolerance using laser equipment. Ensure adequate lubrication flow rate and quality. Implement forced cooling or consider larger bearing if temperatures exceed 70°C.
Continuous temperature monitoring with alarms prevents thermal damage - temperatures above 80°C require immediate shutdown.

What causes severe vibration in 62218 bearings at 3200 RPM?

Severe vibration at 3200 RPM indicates internal damage, severe misalignment, or contamination requiring immediate shutdown.
Why This Matters
High-speed vibration in large bearings indicates serious internal problems that will cause catastrophic failure rapidly. The extended 40mm width makes the bearing sensitive to mounting errors and misalignment. Continued operation risks equipment destruction.
How To Handle It
Stop operation immediately and inspect thoroughly. Check all mounting surfaces, shaft runout, and housing concentricity using precision instruments. Look for contamination, bearing damage, or lubrication failure. Replace bearing if internal damage suspected.
Vibration analysis can differentiate between alignment, contamination, and bearing damage - essential for proper diagnosis.

How do I diagnose problems with 90mm 62218 bearings in critical equipment?

Use comprehensive monitoring including temperature, vibration, ultrasonic, and oil analysis for complete diagnosis.
Why This Matters
The 90mm bore and extreme-duty applications make systematic diagnosis critical for preventing catastrophic failures and equipment destruction. Different diagnostic techniques reveal specific problem types requiring different solutions and intervention timing.
How To Handle It
Establish comprehensive baseline measurements when new. Use thermal imaging, vibration analysis, ultrasonic monitoring, and lubricant analysis. Implement trending software for pattern recognition and failure prediction.
Predictive maintenance programs using multiple diagnostic techniques can predict failures 2-3 months in advance.
