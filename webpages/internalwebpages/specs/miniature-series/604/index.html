⚡Load Ratings

Dynamic Load (Cr) 0.75kN
Static Load (Cor) 0.34kN
Load Capacity 77kg force

💡What This Means

Dynamic Load (0.75kN = 77kg): Maximum force while rotating. Exceeding this causes rapid wear and failure within hours.
Static Load (0.34kN = 35kg): Maximum force when stationary. Use 80% for safety (28kg max recommended).
Calculate Your Load: Measure shaft forces, belt tensions, or gear loads. Include shock/vibration factors.
Failure Warning: Overloading causes bearing raceways to crack and balls to flatten permanently.

🔄Speed Limits

Grease Lubrication 32,000 RPM
Oil Lubrication 40,000 RPM
Recommended Max 28,800 RPM (90%)

💡What This Means

Grease Limit (32,000 RPM): Heat generation increases exponentially above this. Grease breaks down, causing seizure.
Oil Advantage (40,000 RPM): Better heat dissipation allows 25% higher speed. Required for high-speed spindles.
Safe Operating Speed: Use 90% of limit (28,800 RPM) for maximum L10 life of 10,000+ hours.
Failure Mode: Exceeding speed causes cage failure, ball skidding, and catastrophic overheating within minutes.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Expert guidance for 604 bearing applications

🔧 Bearing Selection & Replacement ▼

Is the 604 bearing suitable for my ultra-compact 4mm shaft precision instrument?

Perfect for 4mm shafts requiring 0.75kN load capacity in space-critical applications. Check mounting space and precision requirements match specifications.
Why This Matters
The 604 is the smallest practical bearing for precision applications. Its compact 4×12×4mm design makes it ideal for space-constrained environments like medical devices, precision instruments, and micro-motors where every millimeter counts.
How To Handle It
Verify: 1) Shaft is exactly 4mm diameter, 2) Housing accommodates 12mm outer diameter, 3) You have 4mm width clearance, 4) Loads stay under 0.75kN dynamic/0.34kN static. For precision instruments and micro-mechanisms, the 604 provides optimal space efficiency.
The 604's small size makes installation critical - use proper micro-bearing tools to avoid damage.

Can I substitute a 604 with a larger bearing for higher load capacity?

Size constraints usually prevent substitution. If space allows, 605 or 625 offer higher loads, but verify dimensional compatibility first.
Why This Matters
The 604's ultra-compact design is chosen specifically for space-critical applications. Larger bearings require larger housing modifications and may not fit in precision instruments or miniature devices where the 604 is typically used.
How To Handle It
If 0.75kN is insufficient, consider: 605 (1.05kN, 5×14×5mm) or 625 (1.88kN, 5×16×5mm) if space permits. Alternatively, reduce loads through design changes, use multiple 604s, or specify higher-grade 604 bearings for extended life.
In precision applications, design constraints usually dictate bearing size - optimize the system around the 604's capabilities.
🔨 Installation & Maintenance ▼

What special precautions are needed when installing tiny 604 bearings?

Use specialized micro-bearing tools, clean room conditions, and gentle pressure. Never use hammers or excessive force on small bearings.
Why This Matters
The 604's small size makes it extremely sensitive to installation errors. Improper handling can damage internal geometry, affect precision, or cause premature failure. Micro-bearings require different installation techniques than larger bearings.
How To Handle It
Use micro-bearing installation tools, maintain h6 shaft tolerance (±5μm), work in clean environment, apply gradual pressure through proper tooling. Check for smooth rotation after installation. Contamination is critical at this scale.
For 604 bearings, cleanliness is more important than force - one speck of dust can ruin performance.

How do I properly lubricate such small 604 bearings?

Use sealed bearings (604-ZZ/2RS) when possible. For open bearings, apply minimal high-quality grease with micro-dosing equipment.
Why This Matters
Micro-bearings like the 604 have extremely small internal volumes. Over-lubrication is as damaging as under-lubrication, causing increased friction, overheating, and reduced performance. Precision applications demand precise lubrication.
How To Handle It
Prefer 604-ZZ for most applications. For open bearings, use synthetic grease (NLGI Grade 2), apply micro-doses with precision equipment. Lubrication intervals: every 2000-3000 hours for precision applications. Monitor temperature during operation.
With 604 bearings, less lubrication is often better - aim for thin, even distribution rather than generous application.
🔍 Troubleshooting & Problem Solving ▼

My 604 bearing is causing precision issues in my instrument - what's wrong?

Check for contamination, improper clearance selection, or installation damage. Even microscopic particles affect precision in small bearings.
Why This Matters
Precision instruments rely on bearing accuracy for overall system performance. The 604's small size makes it sensitive to contamination, clearance issues, and installation errors that can introduce unwanted play, vibration, or non-repeatability.
How To Handle It
Systematically check: clearance specification (use C2 for maximum precision), contamination (inspect for particles), installation quality (no damage to races), shaft/housing tolerances (h6/H7), and preload settings if applicable.
For critical precision applications, specify 604 bearings with P5 or P4 precision grades and C2 clearance.

Why do my 604 bearings fail faster than expected in high-speed applications?

Small bearings generate more heat per unit volume. Check speed limits, lubrication type, and cooling provisions for your application.
Why This Matters
The 604's compact size creates thermal challenges at high speeds. Heat generation increases exponentially with speed, and small bearings have less thermal mass to absorb heat. This can cause rapid lubrication breakdown and accelerated wear.
How To Handle It
Stay within speed limits: 32,000 RPM (grease) or 40,000 RPM (oil). Use synthetic lubricants for high-speed operation. Provide adequate cooling/ventilation. Consider 90% speed derating (28,800 RPM) for maximum life. Monitor bearing temperature during operation.
For sustained high-speed operation, oil lubrication and active cooling can extend 604 bearing life significantly.
💰 Cost & Performance Optimization ▼

Are precision-grade 604 bearings worth the extra cost for my application?

For precision instruments and critical applications, yes. Standard grades suffice for general-purpose micro-motors and non-critical mechanisms.
Why This Matters
Precision-grade 604 bearings (P5/P4) cost 2-3x more but offer significantly tighter tolerances and better performance consistency. In precision instruments, the bearing cost is negligible compared to system performance requirements and potential failure costs.
How To Handle It
Use precision grades for: measurement instruments, medical devices, optical equipment, critical positioning systems. Standard P0 grades work for: general motors, fans, non-critical mechanisms, hobby applications. Calculate cost impact of precision vs. system requirements.
In precision applications, bearing failure often costs 100x the bearing price in downtime and recalibration.

How can I maximize 604 bearing life in my miniature application?

Focus on perfect installation, contamination control, appropriate clearance selection, and staying within operating limits. Small bearings are less forgiving.
Why This Matters
The 604's small size makes it particularly sensitive to installation errors, contamination, and operating condition variations. Proper care can extend life from hundreds to tens of thousands of hours, dramatically affecting system reliability and maintenance costs.
How To Handle It
Optimization checklist: Use proper micro-bearing installation tools, maintain ultra-clean environment, select appropriate clearance (C2 for precision, C0 for general use), stay within 0.75kN load and 32,000 RPM limits, use sealed bearings when possible, specify proper shaft/housing tolerances.
For 604 bearings, prevention is everything - proper initial setup prevents 90% of premature failures.
