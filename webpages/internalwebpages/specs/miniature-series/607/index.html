⚡Load Ratings

Dynamic Load (Cr) 2.86kN
Static Load (Cor) 1.12kN
Load Capacity 292kg force

💡What This Means

Dynamic Load (2.86kN = 292kg): Maximum force while rotating. Exceeding this causes rapid wear and failure within hours.
Static Load (1.12kN): Maximum force when stationary. Use 80% for safety.
Calculate Your Load: Measure shaft forces, belt tensions, or gear loads. Include shock/vibration factors.
Failure Warning: Overloading causes bearing raceways to crack and balls to flatten permanently.

🔄Speed Limits

Grease Lubrication 28000 RPM
Oil Lubrication 36000 RPM
Recommended Max 28000 RPM (90%)

💡What This Means

Grease Limit (28000 RPM): Heat generation increases exponentially above this. Grease breaks down, causing seizure.
Oil Advantage (36000 RPM): Better heat dissipation allows higher speed. Required for high-speed spindles.
Safe Operating Speed: Use 90% of limit (28000 RPM) for maximum L10 life of 10,000+ hours.
Failure Mode: Exceeding speed causes cage failure, ball skidding, and catastrophic overheating within minutes.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Expert guidance for 607 bearing applications

Bearing Selection & Replacement ▼

Will a 7mm bearing fit my 7mm shaft properly?

Yes, 607 bearing has exact 7mm bore diameter with h6 tolerance (±0.009mm) for proper interference fit.
Why This Matters
Proper shaft fit prevents bearing slippage and premature failure. Loose fit causes vibration, while tight fit damages bearing races. h6 tolerance ensures optimal interference without excessive stress.
How To Handle It
Measure shaft diameter with micrometer to confirm 7mm ±0.009mm tolerance. Use proper installation tools to avoid bearing damage. For high-precision applications, consider C0 clearance option.
Our 20+ years of manufacturing experience shows h6 shaft tolerance provides optimal performance for 7mm applications.

Can this bearing handle 292 kg of weight?

Yes, 607 bearing has 292 kg (2.86 kN) dynamic load rating with 1.5x safety factor for typical applications.
Why This Matters
Load capacity determines bearing life and reliability. Exceeding load rating causes premature fatigue, increased vibration, and potential bearing failure. Safety factor ensures long service life.
How To Handle It
Calculate actual load including dynamic forces and shock loads. For continuous operation, keep loads under 190 kg. For intermittent use, 292 kg is acceptable with proper lubrication.
Premium Gcr15 steel provides superior load handling vs. standard steel alternatives, extending service life 3-5x.

Is this bearing suitable for 28,000 RPM applications?

Yes, 607 bearing is rated for 28,000 RPM grease lubrication and 36,000 RPM oil lubrication with proper clearance selection.
Why This Matters
Speed rating affects bearing temperature, vibration, and life expectancy. High speeds require proper clearance, lubrication, and balance to prevent overheating and premature failure.
How To Handle It
Use C0 clearance for high-speed applications. Ensure proper lubrication and balance. Monitor temperature during operation. Consider open bearing for maximum speed capability.
Our ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturing includes 100% dimensional testing for optimal high-speed performance.
Installation & Maintenance ▼

How do I install a 7mm bearing without damaging it?

Use proper bearing installation tools and apply force evenly to outer ring only, never to inner ring or balls.
Why This Matters
Improper installation damages bearing races, reduces life expectancy, and causes premature failure. Force applied to inner ring can damage rolling elements and create stress concentrations.
How To Handle It
Use bearing press or installation sleeve. Apply force to outer ring circumference. Ensure shaft and housing are clean and properly sized. Use C0 clearance for precision applications.
Professional installation tools prevent 80% of premature bearing failures and ensure maximum 50,000-hour service life.

What tools do I need for a 292 kg capacity bearing?

For 7mm bearings, use precision tools: bearing press, installation sleeve, micrometer, and proper lubrication equipment.
Why This Matters
Precision tools ensure proper fit and prevent damage. 7mm bearings require careful handling due to small size and high precision requirements. Proper tools prevent costly installation errors.
How To Handle It
Use bearing press with proper adapters. Measure shaft and housing with micrometer (±0.001mm accuracy). Apply thin film of lubricant. Use installation sleeve for even force distribution.
Our manufacturing processes include 100% dimensional testing to ensure perfect fit with standard tools.

How often should I check a bearing running at 28,000 RPM?

Check 607 bearings monthly for vibration, temperature, and noise. High-speed operation requires more frequent monitoring.
Why This Matters
High-speed bearings generate more heat and wear faster. Regular monitoring prevents catastrophic failure and extends service life. Early detection of issues saves costly repairs.
How To Handle It
Monitor vibration levels monthly using V2, V3, V4 standards. Check temperature (should stay under 80°C). Listen for unusual noise. Lubricate as needed.
Premium bearings last 3-10x longer than economy alternatives when properly maintained.

Bearing failure under rated load suggests installation or environmental problems. Proper diagnosis prevents repeat failures and ensures optimal performance. Early intervention saves equipment damage.
How To Handle It
Inspect bearing for contamination, proper fit, and lubrication. Check shaft tolerance (must be h6 ±0.009mm). Verify housing clearance. Monitor vibration levels during operation.

What causes overheating at 28,000 RPM?

Overheating at high speed usually results from insufficient lubrication, excessive clearance, or contamination. Check lubrication quality and bearing condition.
Why This Matters
Overheating accelerates wear, reduces life expectancy, and can cause catastrophic failure. High-speed operation requires optimal lubrication and proper clearance selection.
How To Handle It
Verify lubrication quantity and quality. Check clearance (C0 recommended for high speed). Ensure clean operating environment. Monitor temperature (should stay under 80°C).

How do I diagnose problems with 7mm bearings?
