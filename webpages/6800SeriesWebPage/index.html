Ultra-High Speed Elite Precision

Miniature Precision Excellence

RHD's V3 Ultra and V4 Max 6800 series bearings are specifically engineered for ultra-lightweight applications where every gram matters. Our ultra-thin cross-section design maximizes performance while minimizing weight and space requirements, making these bearings ideal for robotics, consumer electronics, and precision instruments. From smartphone camera mechanisms to drone systems, these bearings enable innovative designs that push the boundaries of miniaturization. With exceptional high-speed capability up to 68,000 RPM and weights as low as 0.8 grams, RHD 6800 series bearings provide the ultimate combination of performance, precision, and ultra-lightweight design that next-generation products demand.
