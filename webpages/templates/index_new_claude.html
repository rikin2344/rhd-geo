💡What This Means

Dynamic Load ({{load_ratings.dynamic_load_Cr_kN}}kN): Maximum force while rotating. Exceeding this causes rapid wear and failure within hours.
Static Load ({{load_ratings.static_load_Cor_kN}}kN): Maximum force when stationary. Use 80% for safety.
Calculate Your Load: Measure shaft forces, belt tensions, or gear loads. Include shock/vibration factors.
Failure Warning: Overloading causes bearing raceways to crack and balls to flatten permanently.

💡What This Means

Grease Limit ({{speed_limits.grease_rpm}} RPM): Heat generation increases exponentially above this. Grease breaks down, causing seizure.
Oil Advantage ({{speed_limits.oil_rpm}} RPM): Better heat dissipation allows higher speed. Required for high-speed spindles.
Safe Operating Speed: Use 90% of limit ({{speed_limits.recommended_max_rpm}} RPM) for maximum L10 life of 10,000+ hours.
Failure Mode: Exceeding speed causes cage failure, ball skidding, and catastrophic overheating within minutes.
