FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Expert guidance for 608 bearing applications

🔧 Bearing Selection & Replacement ▼

How do I know if the 608 bearing is the right choice for my 8mm shaft application?

Perfect for 8mm shafts requiring 3.32kN load capacity. Check shaft diameter, housing space, and load requirements match specifications.
Why This Matters
The 608 is the most popular bearing in electronics and small motor applications. Wrong bearing selection leads to premature failure, equipment damage, and costly downtime. Proper sizing ensures optimal performance and maximum service life in precision applications.
How To Handle It
Verify: 1) Shaft is exactly 8mm diameter, 2) Housing accommodates 22mm outer diameter, 3) You have 7mm width clearance, 4) Loads stay under 3.32kN dynamic/1.38kN static. For electronics and precision instruments, the 608 provides the ideal balance of size, capacity, and reliability.
The 608 is the world's most popular bearing - if it fits your specs, it's likely the right choice.

Can I use a cheaper bearing instead of the 608, or does it need to be exact?

Dimensions must match exactly (8x22x7mm), but quality matters significantly. Cheaper bearings fail 3-5x faster, costing more in downtime.
Why This Matters
Quality differences in 608 bearings are substantial. Cheap bearings use inferior steel, poor heat treatment, and loose tolerances. While initial cost is lower, total cost of ownership is much higher due to frequent replacements, downtime, and potential equipment damage.
How To Handle It
Invest in quality 608 bearings with Gcr15 steel and precision manufacturing. Calculate total cost including replacement labor, downtime, and potential equipment damage. For critical applications, premium bearings pay for themselves through extended life and reliability.
Our 20+ years experience shows quality 608 bearings last 3-10x longer than cheap alternatives.
🔨 Installation & Maintenance ▼

Why do my 608 bearings keep failing early, and how can I prevent it?

Most failures come from hammer installation, wrong tolerances, overloading, or poor lubrication. Focus on proper installation and staying within specifications.
Why This Matters
608 bearings in precision applications operate continuously under varying conditions. Improper installation damages internal geometry, while exceeding specifications causes accelerated wear. Small bearings are particularly sensitive to installation errors and contamination.
How To Handle It
Prevention checklist: Use proper bearing pullers (never hammer), maintain h6 shaft tolerance, stay within 3.32kN load limit, don't exceed 26,000 RPM, re-grease every 6-12 months. Keep installation area clean and use proper tools for precision work.
Most 608 bearing failures are installation-related - invest in proper tools and training.

How often should I re-lubricate my 608 bearings, and with what type of grease?

Re-grease every 6-12 months or 2000-5000 operating hours. Use lithium complex grease (NLGI Grade 2) for standard applications.
Why This Matters
Proper lubrication is critical for 608 bearings in high-speed precision applications. Wrong grease type or intervals leads to overheating, increased friction, and premature wear. Small bearings are particularly sensitive to lubrication quality.
How To Handle It
Use lithium complex grease (NLGI Grade 2) for temperatures -30°C to +120°C. High-speed applications (26,000 RPM) need synthetic grease. Apply until old grease purges out, then stop - over-greasing causes overheating. Monitor temperature during operation.
Quality grease extends 608 bearing life 2-3x - don't compromise on lubrication.
🔍 Troubleshooting & Problem Solving ▼

My 608 bearing is making noise - is it dangerous and what should I do?

Noise indicates problems but isn't always immediately dangerous. Diagnose quickly: grinding = contamination, squealing = lubrication failure, knocking = severe damage.
Why This Matters
Bearing noise is an early warning system. Different sounds indicate different problems, and early intervention can prevent catastrophic failure. In precision applications, even minor noise can indicate performance degradation that affects equipment accuracy.
How To Handle It
Diagnose immediately: Light humming = normal, grinding = contamination/wear, squealing = lubrication failure, knocking = severe damage. Stop operation if noise is loud or irregular. Clean and re-lubricate first - if noise persists, replace bearing before catastrophic failure.
In precision applications, any unusual noise warrants immediate attention - don't wait for complete failure.

What causes 608 bearings to overheat and how do I fix it?

Common causes: excessive speed (over 26,000 RPM), overloading (above 3.32kN), poor lubrication, tight clearance, or misalignment.
Why This Matters
Overheating rapidly degrades bearing materials, reduces lubrication effectiveness, and can cause thermal expansion leading to seizure. Small bearings like the 608 are particularly sensitive to temperature due to their compact design and high-speed capabilities.
How To Handle It
Check immediately: Speed (stay under 26,000 RPM), load (below 3.32kN), lubrication quality and quantity, shaft/housing tolerances, and alignment. Operating temperature should stay under 70°C. Address root cause before continuing operation.
Temperature monitoring is critical for 608 bearings - invest in infrared thermometers for regular checks.
